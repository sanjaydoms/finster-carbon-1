09 / THE GROUP

Sixty-eight years of material experience.

The group traces its origin to 1957 in the USA and began Indian operations in 2009. Masterbatch Technologies LLP manufactures and exports carbon black powder and masterbatch; Finster Black Pvt. Ltd. operates the tyre-recovery and pyrolysis platform at Khalapur, Raigad. Together they cover the route from carbon to colour and from waste back to resource.
