TECHNOLOGY

Carbon, formulation and recovery.

Three capabilities sit behind the product range: the material science of carbon, the manufacturing that turns it into a usable concentrate, and the recovery platform that brings end-of-life tyres back as feedstock.

01 / MATERIAL SCIENCE

What the materials actually are.

CARBON BLACK

Structure and surface

Furnace blacks are specified by ASTM designation — N330, N550, N660 — which fixes the particle size and structure that determine reinforcement, jetness and processing behaviour in a compound.

Grade-level properties: see product pages
MASTERBATCH

Carrier and loading

Black masterbatches are high concentrations of carbon black, typically 15–50%, dispersed in a thermoplastic resin that acts as carrier or binder. Carrier choice governs compatibility with the customer's base polymer.

Carriers supplied: LDPE · LLDPE · virgin LLDPE
RECOVERED CARBON BLACK

A second life for carbon

rCB is the solid output of tyre pyrolysis. It functions as a semi-reinforcing filler with a lower carbon footprint than petroleum-based carbon black, and is blended with or substituted for virgin grades.
