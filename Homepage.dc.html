08 / ABOUT

Building better materials through experience, engineering and circularity.

Two operating identities sit under the group. Masterbatch Technologies LLP, established in 2009, manufactures and exports black carbon powder, LDPE, LLDPE and virgin LLDPE masterbatch, UV stabilizer masterbatch and plastic black masterbatch. Finster Black Pvt. Ltd. operates the tyre-recovery and pyrolysis platform in Raigad, Maharashtra, producing recovered carbon black, tyre pyrolysis oil and crumb rubber.
