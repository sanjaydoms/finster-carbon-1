OUR STORY

Sixty-eight years of material experience

The group traces its origin to 1957 in the United States and began Indian operations in 2009. Today it manufactures carbon black and masterbatch, and operates a tyre-recovery and pyrolysis platform at Khalapur, Raigad — covering the route from carbon to colour and from waste back to resource.
