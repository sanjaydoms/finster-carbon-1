ABOUT

Building better materials through experience, engineering and circularity.

Two operating identities sit under the group. Masterbatch Technologies LLP manufactures and exports carbon black powder and masterbatch. Finster Black Pvt. Ltd. operates the tyre-recovery and pyrolysis platform. Together they cover the route from carbon to colour and from waste back to resource.

The group sells to plastics processors, rubber compounders, tyre manufacturers, pipe and film extruders, and industrial formulators. The commercial conversation is technical: specification, dispersion behaviour, let-down ratio and documentation.
